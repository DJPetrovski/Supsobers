Blok Bottom

Fit your colored pieces onto the board. Every new piece must touch one of your own at a corner, never along a side. Block everyone else in and use up as many squares as you can.

How to play
  • Your first piece must cover your starting square (marked on the board).
  • After that, each piece must touch one of your own pieces at a corner. Never share a side with your own color. Other colors can touch you anywhere.
  • You can’t move a piece after you place it, so plan your corners. When nothing of yours fits, you’re out.
  • Score: minus one for every square still in your hand. Place all 21 pieces for +15, and +5 more if the single square goes last.
  • Online: tables fill up with other visitors. If you don’t want to wait, the host can start early and the computer plays the empty seats.

    How to play

    • Throw: pull the glowing disc back and let go, like a slingshot. The further you pull, the harder and further it goes.
    • Hyzer and anhyzer: pull it back straight down for a flat throw. Pull back to the right and it flies out on a hyzer (fades left). Pull back to the left and it flies on an anhyzer (curves right). The tilt shows as you pull. A full-power pull shakes a little, just like a real arm.
    • Aim and nose: the three bars above the buttons set aim, nose angle (how high it launches) and a power limit so you cannot overthrow. You can also drag anywhere that is not the disc to turn the view.
    • Disc: tap the disc at the bottom left. Drivers go far, putters go short and straight. The Backhand button flips to a forehand throw.
    • Flyover: each hole starts with a quick fly along the line to the basket, then drops you back at the tee. Tap to skip, or switch it off in the Menu.
    • Your dog: tap it to give it a pet. Tap rabbits and squirrels too.
    • Putting: inside about 22 m you putt instead. Put your finger down, pull back toward you, then push forward toward the basket. A longer, quicker push putts harder, and the direction of your push is your aim. Pick Straddle or the overhead Pizza putt (cuts through wind) with the pizza button.
    • Water: putters and midranges skip across the water if you throw them flat and fast.
    • Score: hit the chains. Beat par to earn badges and medals.

    Aim: drag the view: side to side points the throw, up and down changes how high it launches. Disc: tap the disc at the bottom left (the four numbers are its real speed | glide | turn | fade). Power: hold THROW and let go. Use Max to cap your power so you can't overthrow, it resets after each throw. Tune holds the hyzer, anhyzer, nose angle and aim sliders. The dotted line shows where your setup lands. Wind, trees, bushes, boulders and water are real, and to score you have to hit the chains. Beat par to earn badges and medals.
