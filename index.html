Wagon Run

Ride a little red wagon from hill to hill. Hold B (or press and hold the screen) to dive and gain speed. Press Shift (or the JUMP button) to jump. Don\u2019t fall off the wagon.

Hold B (or press and hold the screen) to dive and gain speed. Let go near the top of a hill to launch, and tap Shift (or the JUMP button) to jump. Grab the gold chips. It starts calm and slowly gets steeper, shorter and rockier. If you fall off the wagon, climb back on.

Hole done

Pick a disc (the numbers are speed | glide | turn | fade, like real discs). Drag the view or use the Aim slider to point it. Hyzer tilts the disc so it curves left (backhand) and anhyzer so it curves right. Hold the throw button (or the space bar) to charge power and let go to throw. Wind, trees and water are real, so don’t fall in the drink. Get it in the chains.
