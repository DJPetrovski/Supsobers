September 10 Archive

A chronological, source-linked record of video and photo coverage of the assassination of Charlie Kirk on September 10, 2025, and the days that followed — built so researchers and journalists can move through the case in the order it happened rather than however a search engine surfaces it. The Compare angles tab lines up raw clips on the moment of the shot so angles can be compared. Every entry links out to its original source; where a broadcast has an Internet Archive TV News copy, that's listed too, so a link going dead on the original site doesn't take the record with it.

Submit a video for the archive

Every submission is reviewed by a person before anything is added. The archive does not accept AI-generated or AI-enhanced video. Restoring a file to its original quality without AI (for example, decompressing it) is fine, and we note it. We post what the video shows and who made it, and leave the conclusions to you.

How this works. This page doesn't rehost any video or photo files — every card links to where the footage or image actually lives (a news outlet, a press conference stream, or the Internet Archive's TV News Archive, which permanently and independently preserves U.S. broadcast news for exactly this kind of citation). If a source link goes dead, check the Internet Archive link on that entry before assuming the record is gone.

Entries are sourced from public reporting and official statements. Spotted an error or a broken link? Use the submit form above or email supsobers@gmail.com and it will be corrected.
