Privacy Policy

Supsobers (supsobers.com), run by DJ Petrovski / Sobering Stories. Last updated October 17, 2026.

What this site is

supsobers.com is a free website for the Sobering Stories YouTube channel (@Sobersector). It is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. There are no visitor accounts and no logins.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information from visitors by default. The site does not use advertising trackers. If you choose to send something through a form on the site (for example, suggesting a video for the archive or contacting us), the details you type, such as your message and any email address you add, are sent to us by a form service (Web3Forms) and used only to read and reply to your message.

Your browser may store small settings for the site (for example, which tab you last viewed) on your own device. You can clear these at any time in your browser settings.

Videos and third parties

Videos play from YouTube and other sources. When you press play, that provider may collect data under its own policy (for YouTube, see policies.google.com/privacy). GitHub hosts the site and may log standard visit data such as IP addresses; see GitHub's privacy statement.

YouTube API use

The site owner connects to the YouTube Data API to read the captions of the owner's own channel videos, so the site's search can find what is said in them. This access is used only by the site owner's automated tool, on the owner's own channel. No information about visitors is accessed, collected, or shared through it. Use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell or share personal information. Messages sent through forms are kept only as long as needed to respond.
