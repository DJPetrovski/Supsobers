# Photo archive (data/photos.json)

Array of entries shown on the Photos tab and the front of Browse.

Hosted image you have the rights to:
`{"id":"x1","src":"media/photos/x1.jpg","title":"...","caption":"...","group":"day","date":"2025-09-10","source":"Credit","sourceUrl":"https://original"}`

Publisher embed (the photo is shown from the publisher's own server, not copied):
`{"id":"x2","embed":"https://x.com/user/status/123","title":"...","caption":"...","group":"comparison","source":"Credit"}`
Supported: X/Twitter post URLs, Instagram post/reel URLs, Facebook post URLs, Getty embed URLs (https://embed.gettyimages.com/embed/ID).

group: comparison, fbi, hearing, day, tribute, other
