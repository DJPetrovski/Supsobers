These sounds are from the "Impact Sounds" pack by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0): free to use for anything, no credit required.
Converted from ogg to mono mp3 and renamed: gh/gm/gl = glass heavy/medium/light, wh/wp/wl = wood heavy/plank/light, mh = metal, rk = rock (mining), sf = soft, gn = generic light, lf = grass footsteps (leaves), pn = punch.
To use your own recording for a sound, replace the matching mp3 files (keep the names).
